Leadership

Join the Team

Venture Built is run by students, for students. We're looking for the people who'll help build it.

Apply to the Team See Open Roles

Who We're Looking For

Former exco members, student leaders with a real track record, people genuinely pulled toward the startup or VC world, not just curious about it.

You don't need startup experience. You need to have led something before, and want to build something bigger now.

You've led before. Now help build what comes next.

Open Roles

Four leadership roles, open now.

Head of Platform and Head of Ecosystem are held by our founder for now, these four are open.

01

Head of Programs

Runs everything program-related: the biweekly masterclasses (logistics, scheduling, guest founder coordination) and the events layer, hackathons, monthly Demo Day, the flagship pitch and exhibition. If you can run a session and an event with equal ease, this is yours.

02

Head of Talent

Manages the community end to end, the WhatsApp group and eHub, the co-building space where members find each other and form teams. If you're the person who's always connecting people, this is your role.

03

Head of Growth

Owns the applicant pipeline, our socials, and how people hear about Venture Built in the first place, brand, content, and the wider community's reach. If you think in terms of reach and momentum, this is your role.

04

Head of Finance/Operations

Owns dues, budget, and the paperwork that keeps the club actually running. If you're the person who keeps things organized while everyone else is building, this is yours.

Ready to help run Venture Built?

Four leadership roles are open right now. Tell us what you've led before, and why one of these is yours.
