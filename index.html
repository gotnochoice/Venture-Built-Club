Who We Are

Venture Built is a club for ambitious students who want to build. Not just any company, but one with the potential to become venture-backable.

We bring together students with the ambition to build the next generation of great African startups and give them the knowledge, environment and people to push their ideas further.

Through our flagship 16-week cohort, you will learn what it takes to take an idea from the starting point to something real. You'll test problems, understand your users, build products, challenge your assumptions and learn how to think about your business from an investor's perspective.

You'll build alongside ambitious students, learn from founders, operators and investors, and gain a deeper understanding of the ecosystem around building.

It's intense. It's hands-on. And it's built around two things:

Building. Understanding the venture ecosystem.

Who Is It For?

Venture Built is for ambitious students who want to build something bigger than themselves.

We are looking for exceptional builders from every discipline, from undergraduate to Master's level, who are curious, driven and ready to go all in.

You might have an idea.
You might have a problem you want to solve.
You might have a product already.
Or you might simply know you want to build.

You can apply with a team or on your own.

You don't need a finished startup. You don't need a co-founder. You don't need to have everything figured out.

What matters is that you're willing to go all in, learn fast, test your thinking and do the work.

You just need the ambition, the drive to go all in, and the hunger to learn.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be at OAU to be part of Venture Built?

^

Full cohort membership currently runs at OAU. Our webinars, virtual guest founder sessions, and WhatsApp community are open to student builders anywhere, and that's how we plan to grow to new campuses.

Do I need to already have a startup idea?

^

No. Coming with a rough idea, or even just curiosity about becoming a builder, is a great place to start. What matters is that you're ready to actually put in the work once you're in.

Do I need to know how to code?

^

No. Venture Built is open to every department. If you can't build the technical side yourself, the Co-Builders space is exactly where you find someone who can.

What if I don't get in this time around?

^

You're welcome to apply again next cohort. We'd rather you come back with more clarity than not apply at all.

Does Venture Built invest in my startup?

^

No. We don't fund or take equity in member startups. Our job is to prepare you and connect ready teams to programs and investors who do.
