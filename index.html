What We Do

Learn What's Venture-Backable

Every two weeks, we break down what it actually takes, idea validation to fundraising, taught by club leadership and guest founders joining virtually.

Find Your Co-Builder

Through eHub, our co-building space, list what you're working on and what you need, no cold directory, just people finding each other.

Build Toward Something Real

Team pitches every two weeks, a monthly Demo Day, and one flagship pitch event closing out each 4-month cohort.

Get Connected When Ready

We don't fund or take equity. Once your team is ready, we connect you to the mentors and partners who can take it further.

Who Is Venture Built For?

Open to every department and level, undergraduate or Masters. We don't filter by skill set, whether your edge is engineering, business, marketing, or design. What we're looking for is vision: people who've genuinely started thinking through a problem worth solving, and have the conviction to build it.

The Cohort

Each cohort runs for one full semester, about four months, with an intake of roughly 50 members. Every two weeks: a masterclass session and team pitches, sometimes taught by a guest founder joining virtually. In between: eHub, the WhatsApp community, and milestones tailored to where each team actually is. Once a month: a bigger Demo Day. At the end: one flagship pitch event where the whole cohort presents what they've built.

The Terms

Venture Built does not take equity and does not charge fees to join. We don't invest in or fund member startups directly. Our job is to prepare you and connect ready teams to the mentors, grants, and partners who can take it further.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be at OAU to be part of Venture Built?

^

Full cohort membership currently runs at OAU. Our webinars, virtual guest founder sessions, and WhatsApp community are open to student builders anywhere, and that's how we plan to grow to new campuses.

Do I need to already have a startup idea?

^

No. Coming with a rough idea, or even just curiosity about becoming a builder, is a great place to start. What matters is that you're ready to actually put in the work once you're in.

Do I need to know how to code?

^

No. Venture Built is open to every department. If you can't build the technical side yourself, the Co-Builders space is exactly where you find someone who can.

What if I don't get in this time around?

^

You're welcome to apply again next cohort. We'd rather you come back with more clarity than not apply at all.

Does Venture Built invest in my startup?

^

No. We don't fund or take equity in member startups. Our job is to prepare you and connect ready teams to programs and investors who do.
