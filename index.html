Who we are

Exceptional talent is everywhere. The opportunity to build isn't.

Across African campuses, students have the ability and the ideas. Many don't have the knowledge, the collaborators, or access to people who have built before.

Venture Built closes that gap. We bring students together, grow their skills through learning and hands-on building, and connect them with founders, operators and investors.

Start wherever you are, through our Community, Club or Cohort.

Who it's for

Potential isn't defined by what you've already built.

You might have an idea, a problem you care about, a skill you want to put to use, or a hunger to understand how ambitious products get built. You don't need a startup, a finished product or a co-founder.

The building journey

From curiosity to something built

Everyone starts somewhere different. Go as far as you want.

  1. 01

    Start with what pulls you.

    Bring an idea, a problem, a skill or a question.

  2. 02

    Understand the problem.

    Find out who has it, how they cope today, and whether it's worth solving.

  3. 03

    Shape a solution.

    Challenge your assumptions, compare approaches and decide what to build.

  4. 04

    Test with real people.

    Put it in front of users and teammates, and learn what doesn't work.

  5. 05

    Build and share.

    Turn what works into a product, improve it with feedback and show it publicly.

Join at any step. Not everyone goes through all five.

Frequently asked questions

Who can join, and do I need an idea or a startup?

Undergraduate and postgraduate students who want to build. You don't need a startup, a finished idea or a co-founder. Bring your curiosity, a skill or a problem you care about.

Do I have to be at OAU?

No. The Community is open to students anywhere, including our webinars and virtual founder sessions. The Club and Cohort currently run at OAU. As Venture Built grows, we plan to bring these opportunities to more campuses.

What's the difference between the Community, Club and Cohort?

The Community is for learning and connecting online. The Club is a campus group for exploring and collaborating. The Cohort is a selective, in-person programme for building and testing. You can join any of them first.

Is Venture Built free?

Yes. Every pathway is free to join. We don't charge programme fees or take equity in what you build. We don't invest in member startups. Where it makes sense, we help promising teams prepare and connect them with relevant programmes and investors.

Can I take part if I don't want to be a founder?

Yes. Co-builders, researchers and curious students are part of Venture Built. If you have skills in code, design, research or operations, the Hub's Co-Builders space is where you find people to build with.

What if I don't get into the Cohort?

The Community stays open to you, and you can apply again when applications reopen.

How can founders, investors and organisations get involved?

We welcome founders and operators as mentors, investors who want to meet emerging talent, and partners who support student innovation.
