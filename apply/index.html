Before you apply

Venture Built only accepts the top 1% of applicants. We're not looking for generic answers, and we can tell when a response was written by AI instead of by you. Put real thought into every question, it's the difference between getting in and getting overlooked.

As a member, you'll be learning directly from top OAU alumni founders who've gone on to raise thousands to millions of dollars for their startups. This club is for the best, so give us your best.
