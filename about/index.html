Our Community

Venture Built is home to a growing community of student builders across Africa, working across every industry, from fintech to healthtech to consumer products. As our cohorts graduate, this is where you'll find the ventures they're building.

Right now, we're at the beginning. The founders who go through Venture Built will build this page's story, cohort by cohort. Join the community today and be part of shaping it from the start.
