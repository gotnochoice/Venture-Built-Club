No idea, no product, and no experience needed. We're looking for genuine curiosity and ambition, just come ready to learn and build.

What the Club is

A year-round home for OAU students who want to learn how real businesses get built from the ground up: spotting problems worth solving, meeting other builders, and figuring out your own path into the Cohort. Whether you've never built anything before or you're already tinkering on something small, come as you are.

  • Who it's for: any OAU student, at any stage, complete beginners and experienced builders alike.
  • What you get: monthly meetups, hands-on sessions, a growing knowledge hub, other builders to learn with, and a direct pathway into the Cohort.
  • Review time: within a week of applying.
