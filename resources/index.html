What Members Get Access To

Venture Built isn't just a title, it's a set of tools and people that make building less lonely. Here's what's already running, and what's on the way.

Masterclasses

Every two weeks, we break down what it actually takes to build a venture-backable business, idea validation to fundraising, taught by club leadership and guest founders joining virtually.

eHub

Our co-building platform. List what you're working on and what you need, no cold directory, just people finding each other.

Mentor Connections

We don't fund or take equity. Once your team is ready, we connect you to the mentors and partners who can take it further.

Demo Days & Pitch Events

A monthly Demo Day and one flagship pitch event closing out each 4-month cohort, chances to show what you've built and get real feedback.

Coming soon

A library for builders

We're putting together templates, guides, and recordings for members to reference between sessions. Check back once our first cohort is underway.
