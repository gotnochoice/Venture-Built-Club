Partners

Open Real Doors for Africa's Next Founders

Venture Built partners with companies and organizations who want to help our student founders get their footing. We don't take equity or charge fees, our partners simply help us prepare builders and connect ready teams to the resources that can take them further.

Become a Partner

Who Are Our Partners

Our partners are companies and organizations already close to the work, businesses that have built products, funds that have raised and deployed capital, and institutions that run the programs early founders rely on.

Some teach, sending their people to share a piece of the startup journey firsthand, from idea validation to fundraising. Some open their networks, connecting ready teams to capital, customers, and opportunity. Some show up at Demo Day to see what our builders are putting together and back what they believe in.

What unites them isn't size or sector, it's a willingness to invest a little time or resources in founders who are just getting started.

Ways to Get Involved

Find Your Role in the Ecosystem

Whether your organization can give an hour a month or wants to open real doors for ready teams, there's a place for you at Venture Built.

01

Lead a Webinar

Nominate someone from your team to lead a one-hour webinar. Topics like validating a first version, landing your first customers, how investors read a market, or what raising really involves.

02

Join as a Pitch Reviewer or Judge

A few hours across Demo Day and the Grand Pitch, giving founders real, honest feedback on what they're building.

03

Provide Grants or Funding

Venture Built doesn't take equity or fees itself, but if your organization funds or grants to early-stage African founders, we'll introduce you to teams once they're ready.

04

Send Your Operators

Your team has people with real experience. Nominate them to lead a webinar or review pitches, whatever fits their time.
