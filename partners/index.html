Partners

Our Partners

We're building relationships with mentors, operators, and organizations who want to help Africa's next generation of founders get their footing. Venture Built doesn't take equity or charge fees, our partners help us prepare student builders and connect ready teams to the resources that can take them further.

Ways to Get Involved

Find your role in the ecosystem

Whether you can give an hour a month or want to open real doors for ready teams, there's a place for you at Venture Built.

01

Teach a Masterclass

Join virtually to walk our members through a piece of the startup journey, idea validation to fundraising, from your own experience.

02

Fundraising Mentorship

Support ready teams preparing to raise, through pitch coaching and Demo Day judging. (Upon eligibility and interest.)

03

Provide Grants or Funding

Venture Built doesn't take equity or fees itself, but if you fund or grant to early-stage African founders, we'll introduce you to teams once they're ready.

04

Back a Demo Day

Show up at a monthly Demo Day or our flagship pitch event and see what Great Ife's next builders are putting together.
