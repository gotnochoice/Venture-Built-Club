Our Partners

Venture Built connects ambitious student builders across Africa with the companies and firms who can help take their ventures further. As a partner, you get early access to the founders coming out of our program, before they're on anyone else's radar.

Your support funds the masterclasses, the pitch days, and the flagship event that make the program run, we don't take equity or dues, so partnerships and sponsorships are what keep this going.

We're building our partner network now. If you want to help shape the next generation of African founders, reach out.
