Our Community

Venture Built is home to a growing community of student builders across Africa, starting at Obafemi Awolowo University. Through eHub, our co-building platform, and biweekly masterclasses, our members share feedback, sharpen ideas, and find the co-builder who completes them. We're just getting started, real numbers on our community will go here once we have them to share. Explore where we're building →

eHub

List what you're working on and what you need. No cold directory, just people finding each other and building together.

Masterclasses

Biweekly sessions on what it actually takes to build a venture-backable business, taught by club leadership and guest founders.

Demo Days

A monthly chance to show what you and your peers have built, and get real feedback from people who've been there.
